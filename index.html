Dentistry

Overview/Introduction/Context


There had been many Technological advancements that had impacted the field of dentistry. Stating this, each invention was able to improve research for more affordable and reliable dental tools, which can be used by all dental professionals for a more efficient workplace. By looking at the timeline and purposes of these inventions, we are able to better understand the way technology is used in today's world to improve the fields of dental and oral health.

Part A

ABELDent is dental software used for managing patient records, appointments, and billing. It helps dental staff organize their daily work smoothly. It's useful because it makes dental office tasks easier and helps ensure patients get timely care.

Dental Intelligence is a software that helps dental offices manage appointments, payments, and patient records. It makes work easier and helps dentists keep track of their practice’s performance. It's useful because of the fact that it saves time, improves efficiency, and helps dentists provide better care by organizing important information.

Dental Monitoring uses AI and a smartphone app to check orthodontic treatment progress remotely. Patients take scans at home, and the AI helps orthodontists see how treatment is going.It reduces in-office visits, saves time, and allows personalized treatment updates.

Diagnocat is software that looks at dental X-rays and 3D scans to find problems like cavities or bone issues. It makes reports and 3D models to help plan treatments. It helps dentists find and explain problems easily, making diagnosis faster and more accurate.

An autoclave is a machine that uses steam and heat to sterilize dental tools and equipment.Its useful because it kills germs and bacteria, keeping dental tools safe and clean for patients

SingLED Operatory Light is a bright LED light used in dental chairs to give dentists clear, shadow-free light during procedures.It's useful due to the fact that it helps dentists see better also reducing eye strain during long treatments.

Dental Suction Machine is a suction machine removes saliva, blood, or other fluids from a patient's mouth during dental work.This tool keeps the mouth dry and clear, making procedures easier and more comfortable.

Uniz NBEE 3D Printer - Voxel Edition is a 3D printer of which makes dental models, crowns, or aligners by printing layer by layer from resin.This speeds up creating precise dental parts and models for treatment.
