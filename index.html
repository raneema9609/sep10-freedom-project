Overview/Introduction/Context

There had been many technological advancements that impacted the field of dentistry. Each invention improved research for more affordable and reliable dental tools, which can be used by all dental professionals for a more efficient workplace. By looking at the timeline and purposes of these inventions, we better understand how technology is used in today's world to improve dental and oral health.

Part A: Existing Technology

Dental Intelligence

Dental Intelligence is a software that helps dental offices manage appointments, payments, and patient records. It makes work easier and helps dentists keep track of their practice’s performance. It saves time, improves efficiency, and helps dentists provide better care by organizing important information.

Dental Monitoring

Dental Monitoring uses AI and a smartphone app to check orthodontic treatment progress remotely. Patients take scans at home, and the AI helps orthodontists see how treatment is going. It reduces in-office visits, saves time, and allows personalized treatment updates.

Diagnocat

Diagnocat analyzes dental X-rays and 3D scans to find problems like cavities or bone issues. It creates reports and 3D models to help plan treatments. It makes diagnosis faster and more accurate.

Autoclave

An autoclave sterilizes dental tools using steam and heat. It kills germs and bacteria, keeping tools safe and clean for patients.

SingLED Operatory Light

This bright LED light provides shadow-free illumination during dental procedures, helping dentists see better and reducing eye strain.

Dental Suction Machine

Removes saliva, blood, or other fluids during dental work. Keeps the mouth dry and procedures comfortable.

Uniz NBEE 3D Printer - Voxel Edition

Creates dental models, crowns, or aligners by printing layer by layer from resin, speeding up treatment preparation.
